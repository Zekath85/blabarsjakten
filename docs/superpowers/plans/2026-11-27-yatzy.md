# Yatzy Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** A new game, Yatzy, in Spelhörnan. 1–4 players or play against Robo. Each player has their own scoresheet, and the dice are thrown from a shaker cup into a tray.

**Architecture:** Same pattern as `www/games/fiamedknuff/`.
- `index.html` borrows `../blabarsjakten/game.css`.
- `game.js` holds the screens.
- `logic.js` is pure and tested.

New here is `dice.js`, which precomputes the throw of five dice in a tray (pure, tested). The screens are copied from Fia med knuff (choose players, the picker with level and rounds, rules, sound panel, win panel).

**Tech Stack:** Vanilla HTML/CSS/ES modules, no build step. Web Audio through `shared/audio.js`. Tests with `node:assert` in `test/logic.test.js` (`npm test`). Voices from ElevenLabs via `scripts/make-voices-eleven.mjs`.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-27-yatzy-design.md`

## Global Constraints
- Only static files under `www/`. No new dependencies. Works offline. Uses relative paths.
- All text in the UI is in Swedish.
- No emoji. Icons come from `shared/icons.js` or are drawn in SVG.
- Swedish Yatzy rules:
  - 15 rows. The bonus is 50 at an upper sum of 63 or more.
  - Small straight is 1-2-3-4-5 = 15. Large straight is 2-3-4-5-6 = 20. Yatzy = 50.
  - Five of a kind counts as Three of a kind and Four of a kind, but **not** as Full house or Two pairs.
- A throw takes about 1.5–2 s from release to the dice lying still. A tap fast-forwards to the end state.
- Robo has three levels: `easy`, `normal` and `hard` (shown as Lätt/Medel/Svår). "Först till" is 1, 2 or 3 won matches.
- The game must never point out a single "best" choice. The help has three levels: `none`, `some` and `lots` (shown as Ingen/Lite/Mycket), set per seat.
- Storage:
  - Names and figures live in `bbj-players`. Always save by merging: `{ ...old, names, looks, count, bot, botName }`.
  - The game's own choices live in `yatzy-prefs`. The ongoing match lives in `yatzy-save`.
- Calm mode (`settings.calm`) means fewer and shorter animations.
- Phone layout at `max-width: 600px`. The tablet is in landscape.

## Review Focus
1. **Five of a kind** must not count as Full house or Two pairs. The test is in Task 1.
2. **Tapping during the throw or Robo's turn:** taps on the sheet and the dice must be ignored, and nothing may be scored twice. Tested in the browser in Task 6. In code, a `busy` guard.
3. **All five dice held:** the cup and "Kasta" are disabled until a die is released. Test in Task 5.
4. **Resuming a saved match:** the sheets, the turn, the won matches and the copies of names come back exactly. `fromSave(toSave(g))` round-trips. Test in Task 1.
5. **A 0-point row:** it is asked about first ("Stryka X?"). If the answer is No, nothing changes. Tested in the browser in Task 4.

### Task 2: Help stars and Robo (`logic.js`)

**Files:**
- Modify: `www/games/yatzy/logic.js`, `test/logic.test.js`

**Interfaces:**
- Consumes: `score`, `left`, `UPPER` from Task 1.
- Produces:
  - `LEVELS = ['easy','normal','hard']`, `HELP = ['none','some','lots']`
  - `goodRows(sheet, dice) -> string[]`: 0, 2 or 3 ids, never 1, so the stars never point at a single answer.
    - A free row counts as good when `score(id, dice)` is ≥ its "normal value" in `TYPICAL`.
    - `TYPICAL` is a fixed table: upper row = 3 × face, `pair` 8, `twoPairs` 14, `three` 12, `four` 13, `small` 15, `large` 20, `house` 18, `chance` 22, `yatzy` 50.
    - Sort by `score/TYPICAL`, highest first, and take at most 3. If exactly 1 qualifies, add the next best row with a score > 0. If none has a score > 0, return `[]`.
  - `botHold(sheet, dice, level, rnd=Math.random) -> boolean[5]`: which dice to keep.
    - `easy`: keep the most common face.
    - `normal`: also chase a straight when 4 dice are in sequence and `small` or `large` is free.
    - `hard`: choose the hold pattern that gives the highest mean of `best free score` over 200 simulated rerolls with `rnd`.
  - `botPick(sheet, dice, level, rnd=Math.random) -> id`
    - `easy`: a random free row with a score > 0 half the time, otherwise the highest-scoring free row.
    - `normal`/`hard`: the highest `score/TYPICAL`. When everything is 0, strike the free row with the lowest `TYPICAL`.

### Task 3: The throw of five dice (`dice.js`)

**Files:**
- Create: `www/games/yatzy/dice.js`
- Modify: `test/logic.test.js`

**Interfaces:**
- Produces:
  - `DT = 1/60`
  - `FACE`: the same table as in `blabarsjakten/game.js:516`.
  - `simulateTray(starts:{x,y,vx,vy}[], tray:{l,t,r,b}, size:number, rnd) -> { frames: {x,y,z,rx,ry,knock}[][], length:number }`
    - One frame list per die, all of the same length.
    - Bounce against the tray edges (inset by `size/2`) and between dice, using circle collision with diameter `size` and swapped normal velocity × 0.8.
    - Gravity and roll as in `simulateThrow` in Blåbärsjakten.
    - Stop when all dice are still. Hard cap at 150 frames (2.5 s). After that, slow down hard so that the dice stop at most 30 frames later.
  - `startRotation(face:1-6, last:{rx,ry}, rnd) -> {rx,ry}`: the same trick as `throwDice`, so the die ends exactly on `face`.
